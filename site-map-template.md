# Site Map Template: Photography Guide 

## Site Purpose: The purpose of this website is to educate beginner photographers and to serve as a guide for others to revisit. 


## Primary Audience: The audience will be photographers or anyone interested in photography. 


## Primary User Tasks

1. Understand the different parts of the camera.
2. Understand the key settings to operate the camera more effectively. 
3. View tutorials

## Proposed Site Map

- Home
  - Purpose: An introduction to the website with a brief overview of what you can learn. 
  - Key content: Information and a hero image to engage visitors. 
- Camera 101
  - Purpose: Information on different camera parts and their function. Here, users learn how to use their camera. 
  - Key content: Important information about camera parts, additional imagery or charts that support the information given, and possibly a linked video. 
- Dial It In
  - Purpose: Explanations of settings such as ISO, aperture, shutter speed, and exposure. 
  - Key content: Important information about camera settings, additional imagery or charts that support the information given, and possibly a linked video. 
- Tips and Tutorials
  - Purpose: Helpful camera tips along with videos to help photographers progress in the field. 
  - Key content:  Information and linked videos. 

## Content Risks to Resolve

1. Giving accurate information as well as information that is not too broad or too niche. 
2. Adding the right amount of information to keep visitors engaged. 
3. Video and images copyright issues.
